# v0.9 demo handoff

Release scope: one evidence-to-action experience for builders and ecosystem decision-makers.

## What changed

- Six demo entry questions now converge on the same structured analysis contract.
- Builder questions expose relevant requirements, production stages, candidate organizations or sourced facilities, evidence limits, and next verification steps.
- Ecosystem questions expose current documentation coverage and validation priorities without treating record counts as market capacity or proof of a gap.
- The response workspace drives the existing map, process graph, organization registry, facility records, and program records through validated IDs and actions.
- The page includes an explicitly work-in-progress ecosystem-intelligence preview for SoCal Deep Tech, agencies, funders, buyers, and conveners.
- Questions are shareable with a `?q=` URL.
- `data/question_corpus.json` contains 30 builder, company, buyer, ecosystem, and adversarial questions. Automated tests audit the six anchors plus schema, citation, and prohibited-claim boundaries across the full corpus.

## Evidence boundary

The release contains no synthetic organizations or facilities. It reuses 250 sourced discovery records, 20 individually sourced facilities/sites, 25 evidence dossiers, 14 public-engineering process stages, and seven governed program/ecosystem records. Historical or secondary records remain labeled. Documentation coverage is not supply adequacy; a missing record is not evidence that a capability is absent.
